The web does not need another dashboard pretending to be a place. Sometimes a page can simply hold a thought, give it enough space to breathe, and let the reader leave when they are done.

<!--more-->

I keep returning to the small personal site: fast to load, gentle on the eyes, and built from pieces that will likely still make sense in ten years. It is a useful constraint. Fewer moving parts means more attention for the words and the work.

That is what I want this notebook to be: a durable, quiet corner of the internet.
